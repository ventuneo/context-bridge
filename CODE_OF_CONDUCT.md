# Code of Conduct

Context Bridge follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, is
expected to follow it.

In short:

- Be respectful and constructive, and assume good intent.
- Welcome newcomers and people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people.
- Do not harass or insult anyone, do not use sexualised or discriminatory language, and do not
  publish other people's private information.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately with GitHub's **Report content** option. It is in the menu
of the comment, issue or pull request. The maintainers review every report and keep the identity
of the reporter confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break this code. They may also block
people temporarily or permanently, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor
Covenant.
